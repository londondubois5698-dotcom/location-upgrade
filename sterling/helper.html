Sterling Helper V11

One small bookmark. After this setup, it always loads the newest helper automatically. V11 can pair a customer phone to your Salesforce iPad with a private QR relay.

iPhone / iPad — 3 steps

1

Tap Copy Sterling Bookmark.

2

Tap Safari Share → Add Bookmark. Name it Sterling Helper.

3

Open Bookmarks → Edit → Sterling Helper. Delete the normal web address and paste the code you copied into the URL field. Tap Done.

From then on: open the active ICL house, tap the Sterling Helper bookmark, then tap Use Customer Phone. Let the customer scan the QR. Sterling sends the confirmed contact back to your iPad automatically.

Desktop

Sterling Helper

What changed

The bookmark is only a tiny loader. The real helper updates itself. V11 reads the current address actually visible at the top of the Salesforce record and ignores Previous Opportunity and Next Opportunity addresses. If Salesforce changes houses without changing the old URL, the visible current-house address wins.

When a confirmed customer packet comes back and matches the active house, Auto mode can fill the four contact fields, save, open Order, and capture the Partner Order ID.

Manual code

Safari does not allow a website to create or edit your bookmark for you. That one manual bookmark edit is an Apple browser restriction. After it is saved, V10 updates itself from the web.
